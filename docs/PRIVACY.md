# Privacy Policy – Minnal pages (Kovai Minnal, Chennai Minnal and other "Minnal" news pages)

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The Minnal pages (for example கோவை மின்னல் / Kovai Minnal and சென்னை மின்னல் / Chennai Minnal) publish short local news videos on
Instagram and Facebook. They are run by Minnal Media. The Meta app "Minnal Pages" is used only to publish our own posts on our own
Instagram accounts and Facebook pages, and to read the comments and statistics of those posts.

## What we collect

- We do not ask the public for any personal information and we do not run a sign-up, login or payment.
- When you comment on, message or react to our posts, Instagram / Facebook show that to us like to any page owner. We use it only to
  read and answer comments and to understand which news is useful. We do not sell, rent or share it.
- Our news content is written from public news sources. We do not collect data about the people in the news beyond what those public
  reports contain.

## How long we keep it

Comments and statistics stay on Instagram / Facebook under their own rules. Our own records of post statistics are kept only as long
as needed to improve the pages.

## Your choices and data deletion

- You can delete your own comments or messages at any time on Instagram / Facebook.
- To ask us to delete anything related to you (for example a comment we stored while answering it, or a correction to a news post),
  e-mail **kovaiminnalnews@gmail.com** with the link to the post or comment. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.
